Notes on the sizes

  • itch.io cover (630×500) is the image shown in browse grids and on your game's embed card. itch accepts other sizes but crops to this ratio, so it's better to choose the crop yourself.
  • CrazyGames asks for several covers in different orientations. Keep the game title readable at small sizes and leave some breathing room at the edges.
  • Poki thumbnails show up from huge to tiny. At 204×204, check that the main character or object still reads.
  • Steam capsule sizes are listed for convenience. Valve's rules about text and logos on capsules matter as much as the pixel size.

Portals update their requirements from time to time. Before you submit, check the platform's current developer docs. If a size is missing or wrong here, the custom size box covers you in the meantime.

Tips for good crops

  • Start from art at least as big as the largest size you need (1920 px wide for the landscape covers). Upscaling makes things soft.
  • Square and portrait crops cut a lot from a landscape image. Move the focus sliders until the subject sits in the middle of every thumbnail.
  • For pixel art, scale the source up by a whole number first, or use Contain mode with a solid pad colour to avoid blurry, uneven pixels.
  • JPG at 85–92 quality is usually far smaller than PNG for painted or 3D art. Stick with PNG for flat colours and crisp pixel art.
